Numerology: three ways to turn a name or a date into numbers

Pythagorean numerology.

The alphabet is written out in order and numbered 1 to 9, then 1 to 9 again. A is 1, I is 9, J goes back to 1. Add the numbers of your name and keep adding the digits until one is left.

What it needs
Your full name at birth, and your date of birth.
What it gives
Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge, Personality, Birth number and Personal Year.
In your report
The five large numbers at the top, and the Numerology reading.

Chaldean numerology.

An older system that numbers letters by their sound, not their place in the alphabet, and uses only 1 to 8. No letter is given 9. The two-digit total before reducing, the compound number, is read as well.

What it needs
The name you are known by. Chaldean readers often use the everyday name.
What it gives
Name number with its compound, Soul Urge and Personality.
In your report
Shown beside the Pythagorean numbers, with a note on where the two agree.

The Lo Shu grid.

A three-by-three square from China in which every row, column and diagonal adds to 15. The digits of your birth date are placed on their own squares. Numbers that repeat are read as strengths; empty squares as qualities to build.

What it needs
Your date of birth only.
What it gives
Which of 1 to 9 are present, repeated or missing.
In Destiny OS
Used in our early research. It is not part of your report today.

The Vedic chart: five things read from the sky at your birth

Rashi: the sidereal zodiac.

The Sun's yearly path is cut into twelve signs of 30° each. Western astrology ties the signs to the seasons. Jyotisha ties them to the fixed stars. The Earth's axis slowly wobbles, so the two have drifted about 24° apart, and they keep drifting by one degree every 72 years.

What it needs
Date, time and place of birth.
What it gives
The sign each planet was in. Your Rashi is the sign of the Moon.
How we calculate it
Swiss Ephemeris positions, shifted with the Lahiri ayanamsa.

Nakshatra: the Moon's 27 houses.

The same circle is also cut into 27 parts of 13°20′ each. The Moon crosses one roughly every day. The one it was in when you were born is your birth nakshatra, and it decides where your Dasha periods begin.

What it needs
The Moon's position, so date and time matter.
What it gives
One of 27 names, from Ashwini to Revati, each with a ruling planet.
In your report
In the Vedic chart reading, marked as a gold arc on the wheel.

Tithi: the lunar day.

A tithi measures how far the Moon has pulled ahead of the Sun. Every 12° is one tithi, so there are 30 in a lunar month: 15 while the Moon grows (Shukla paksha, ending in Purnima, the full Moon) and 15 while it shrinks (Krishna paksha, ending in Amavasya, the new Moon).

The rule
Moon's longitude minus Sun's longitude, divided by 12, plus one.
What it gives
A number from 1 to 30 and a name, such as Shukla Ekadashi.
In your report
In the Vedic chart reading, with how much of the Moon was lit.

Lagna: the rising sign.

As the Earth turns, the whole zodiac rises in the east and sets in the west once a day. The sign on the eastern horizon at the minute you were born is your Lagna. A new sign rises about every two hours, which is why the birth time matters so much.

What it needs
Exact time and place of birth.
What it gives
Your rising sign, and from it the twelve houses of the chart.
If you have no birth time
We leave the Lagna out. We do not guess it.

Vimshottari Dasha.

A calendar of life in nine periods adding up to 120 years. Your birth nakshatra decides which period you were born into, and how far the Moon had moved through it decides how much of that period was already used up. Each period is split into nine sub-periods in the same proportions.

What it needs
The Moon's exact position at birth.
What it gives
Your Mahadasha and Antardasha now, with their dates.
In your report
The gold strip on every year of the three-year report.

What our research found.

We tested three of these as predictors: numerology numbers, zodiac sign and the phase of the Moon at birth. Added to a model that already knew a person's history, they changed its score from 79.7 to 79.6, which is no change. So we present all of these methods as what they are: traditions with exact rules, offered for reflection. Practical guidance comes from HALIA, which uses none of them.
