How it works

Five steps, repeated every day.

  1. You agree to how your answers are used

    Adults only. This is separate from signing up, and you can withdraw it at any time.

  2. HALIA asks one useful question

    It starts with what you want to improve and how things stand today, then fills in the rest over time. A skipped question rests for a week.

  3. Your answers become your profile

    Each answer is stored with its date. "How are things going" questions are asked again every month, so HALIA sees the direction you are moving in.

  4. Guidance uses only that profile

    Chat replies and comparisons draw on what you declared. Your date of birth, name and chart are never sent to HALIA.

  5. You stay in control

    See every answer HALIA holds, remove any of them, or delete everything in one tap.

The science

Why HALIA asks about your life today.

Before building HALIA we tested what predicts how people's careers and earnings turn out, using public datasets. Three findings shaped the design.

1. Where you are now is the strongest signal

We asked a model to predict whether a person would earn above the median next year. A coin flip scores 50%.

Background only (education, ethnicity)62%
Plus current context (job, hours, household)66%
Plus current level (what they earn today)83%

545 men followed for eight years in a public US wage panel from the 1980s, tested on people the model had not seen. This is why HALIA asks how each part of your life stands right now, and asks again each month.

2. Predictions fade with distance

The same model, looking three to four years ahead instead of one, dropped from 83% to 77%. HALIA therefore stays close to the present: small actions for today and this week, not forecasts for your life.

3. Birth details added nothing measurable

We added numerology numbers, zodiac sign and lunar phase to a model that already knew a person's track record, across about 3,600 held-out seasons of professional baseball careers.

Track record only73.9%
Track record plus real birth details73.0%
Track record plus someone else's birth details73.2%

Outcome: still a regular player three seasons later. Majority guess 53.9%. The differences are within noise. So HALIA is built on what you tell it. Astrology and numerology readings remain a separate, traditional part of Destiny OS and do not feed HALIA.

What published research adds

A study pooling 43 long-running studies of couples found that how each person feels about the relationship itself is what predicts its quality. Personality traits and a partner's own answers added little, and nothing predicted whose relationship would improve or decline. That is why HALIA shows couples where their answers differ and stops short of a verdict.

HALIA's daily rhythm follows the "just-in-time adaptive intervention" approach from health research: small support, fitted to your current situation, at the moment you can use it.

Sources: Joel et al., PNAS, 2020 · Nahum-Shani et al., Annals of Behavioral Medicine, 2018

  • No score until there is enough to go on. Both people must have answered most of the questions for that comparison. Until then HALIA shows how many are left.
  • The differences matter more than the number. Every report lists the questions where you differ most, so you know what to talk about.

The score shows how closely your answers match on what you each said matters. It is not a prediction of how a relationship, partnership or job will turn out.

Limits

What HALIA will not do.

  • Predict events, name lucky or unlucky days, or promise outcomes.
  • Tell you whether to marry, stay with or leave someone.
  • Diagnose a health problem or suggest medicines.
  • Recommend specific investments, loans or trades.
  • Send sensitive questions, such as family plans or faith, on WhatsApp. Those appear only if you open a couple comparison.

If you are in distress, HALIA will point you to Tele-MANAS (14416, free, 24 hours) or emergency services (112).

Not proven yet

What we still have to show.

  • That HALIA's guidance improves people's lives. This needs a controlled study with consenting users, which has not been run.
  • That an alignment score predicts anything. Today it describes how your answers match. We will test it first on job fit, where outcomes can be measured.
  • That our findings hold in India today. The tests above used US earnings data from the 1980s and professional sport.

We will update this page as results come in, including the ones that don't go our way.
