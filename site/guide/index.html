Two ways of seeing, kept apart.

Your report has two kinds of content. They come from different places and should be read differently, so every part is marked with which one it is.

Tradition

Your numerology numbers, your birth chart and your Dasha periods. They are calculated from your name, date, time and place of birth by fixed, documented rules.

Read it as reflection. The calculation is exact; what the result means is a matter of tradition, and it is not a prediction.

HALIA

Where your life stands today and one step to take. It is built only from the answers you give about your work, relationships, money, happiness and health.

Read it as a plan. It uses no birth details, no numerology and no astrology.

Your numbers.

Numerology turns your name and birth date into single digits. Type any name and date to see every step. Nothing you type here is sent or saved.

The letter tables

Each letter has a value. We calculate with both systems and tell you where they agree. The headline numbers in your report use the Pythagorean table.

The rules

  1. To reduce a number, add its digits and repeat until one digit is left. 1994 becomes 1+9+9+4 = 23, then 2+3 = 5.
  2. 11, 22 and 33 are master numbers. For Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge and Personality we stop when we reach one of them.
  3. Soul Urge uses only the vowels of your name (A, E, I, O, U). Personality uses only the consonants.
  4. Personal Year changes every 1 January. It uses your birth day and month with the current year.

Your chart and Dasha.

Your chart is a map of where the Sun, Moon and planets were at the moment and place you were born.

  1. Your birth city gives a latitude, longitude and time zone. Your birth time is converted to universal time.
  2. The position of each planet is computed with the Swiss Ephemeris, a widely used astronomical library built on NASA JPL data.
  3. Positions are shifted to the sidereal zodiac with the Lahiri ayanamsa, the standard used in Jyotisha and by the Indian government's almanac.
  4. The sky is cut into 12 signs of 30° each and 27 nakshatras of 13°20' each. Your Moon sign and birth nakshatra are wherever the Moon falls.
  5. Your Lagna is the sign rising on the eastern horizon. It moves through a sign in about two hours, so it needs your birth time. Without a time we leave it out instead of guessing.

How a Dasha period is calculated

Vimshottari Dasha divides 120 years among nine lords, always in this order and with these lengths:

Ketu 7 years, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17. Together, 120.

  1. Your birth nakshatra decides which lord's period you were born into. The 27 nakshatras repeat the nine lords three times.
  2. How far the Moon had moved through that nakshatra decides how much of that first period had already passed at your birth.
  3. From there the periods follow in order. This long period is your Mahadasha.
  4. Each Mahadasha is split into nine shorter Antardashas in the same proportions, starting with its own lord. Your report names both, for example "Moon–Jupiter".

If you did not give a birth time, the Moon's position can be off by a few degrees, so Dasha dates may shift by up to a few months. Your report says so when that applies.

How HALIA works.

HALIA starts from a finding in our research: the best guide to where your work, money or wellbeing will be next year is where it is today, together with your situation. Your birth date added nothing we could measure. So HALIA asks about today.

  1. You rate five areas of your life from 1 (very low) to 5 (very good): career, relationships, money, happiness and health.
  2. HALIA finds your lowest area. That is where a small change usually helps most, so it becomes your focus. If you tell it what you want to work on, your choice wins.
  3. You say how much time you have each day. HALIA gives one step for your focus that fits that time.
  4. It asks again every 30 days, because your levels change. One short question a day on WhatsApp, if you want it.

The match score.

On HALIA Match, the number on a card is how closely two people's answers agree, from 0 to 100. It compares 13 questions in five areas. Areas that matter more for a shared life count for more:

Life goals9
Home and values5
Money3
Day to day3
Handling disagreement2
  1. For a choice question, such as whether you want children, the same answer scores full marks and a different answer scores none. "Open either way" or "Flexible" agrees with anything.
  2. For a scale question, such as saver to spender, the closer your answers the higher the marks. Neighbouring answers score half.
  3. Each question's marks are multiplied by its weight. The total is divided by the highest total possible for the questions you both answered.
  4. No number is shown until you have both answered at least 6 of the same questions covering 60% of the weight. Until then the card says "New".

How predictable is a life?

Before building HALIA we tested what can and cannot be predicted about a person's next few years. We used three public datasets and asked each model a yes-or-no question about the future, then checked it against what really happened. The score is how many answers out of 100 it got right.

The ladder: what each kind of information adds

The question: will this man earn more than the typical man next year? 545 men, followed from 1980 to 1987. Each step gives the model more to work with.

The big jump comes from one thing: where the person stands today. Four more years of history added nothing on top of that. This is why HALIA asks you to rate your life as it is now.

The two models, and how we kept the test fair

Logistic regression

Gives each fact a weight and adds them up. Simple, and every weight can be inspected. Used for the earnings study.

Gradient-boosted trees

Hundreds of small decision trees, each one correcting the mistakes of the last. It can catch combinations of facts. Used for baseball and the census.

On baseball we ran both. They agreed to within half a point (79.8 and 79.7), which tells us the result comes from the information and not from the choice of model.

  1. Every score is on people the model never saw while learning.
  2. For baseball the model learned from seasons up to 2002 and was tested on 2005 onwards, so it never saw the future.
  3. Every score is compared with plain guessing, so a common outcome cannot flatter the model.
  4. We ran a control with the wrong birthdays, described next.

Does a birth date add anything?

We gave the baseball model numerology numbers, zodiac sign and lunar phase from each player's real birth date. Then we repeated it with another player's birth date swapped in.

Career history only79.7
+ real birth details79.6
+ someone else's79.8

The three bars are the same. The change from adding real birth details was −0.2 points, with a range of −0.5 to +0.1, which includes zero. Birth details on their own scored 75, the same as guessing. So HALIA leaves them out, and your numbers and chart are offered as tradition, for reflection.

Predictions fade with time

The same information is worth about six points less three years out. A life moves. This is why HALIA asks again every 30 days instead of deciding once, and why the three-year report gives steps and not forecasts.

The six phases of HALIA.

Each phase exists because of one of the findings above. The tag shows whether that phase rests on a tested result or is our design choice.

1

Ask where you stand today tested

How: you rate career, relationships, money, happiness and health from 1 to 5.

Why it works: today's level was the strongest single predictor. It lifted the score from 66 to 83.

2

Add your situation tested

How: a few questions on your work, your time and what you want to improve.

Why it works: life context on its own moved the score from 50 to 66, and from 76 to 87 on the census.

3

Leave birth details out tested

How: HALIA never reads your date, time or place of birth.

Why it works: adding them changed nothing: 79.7 without, 79.6 with.

4

Choose one focus and one step design choice

How: your lowest area becomes the focus, unless you pick another. One step, sized to your time.

Why we chose it: one small action is more likely to get done than a long plan. We have not yet measured this on our own users.

5

Ask again every month tested

How: your levels are re-asked every 30 days, one short question at a time.

Why it works: the same answers were worth six points less after three years. Fresh answers keep the picture true.

6

Speak only when enough is known tested

How: a match score needs at least 6 shared answers covering 60% of the weight.

Why it works: answering only when sure raised accuracy from 74 to 90.

Read these scores carefully

They come from public research data on earnings, census income and baseball careers. They show what kind of information predicts a life. They are not a measured accuracy for Destiny OS reports, and HALIA does not show you a prediction. Testing HALIA's steps on our own users, with their consent, is the next stage of the research.

What it cannot tell you.

  • It cannot predict events. No date for a job, a marriage, a child or money.
  • Numerology and astrology are traditions. In our own tests on public data, birth-based numbers did not improve predictions.
  • HALIA only knows what you tell it. If your answers are out of date, so is its advice.
  • A match score compares answers. It knows nothing about chemistry, kindness or effort.
  • Nothing here replaces a doctor, a lawyer, a financial adviser or a counsellor.
