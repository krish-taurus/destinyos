Good to know.

Is the report really free?

Yes. Register with your birth details and phone number and you get one full free report. Destiny Plus at ₹599 a month unlocks the complete report, unlimited questions and daily WhatsApp guidance.

Why both Pythagorean and Chaldean?

They map letters to numbers differently and can give different results. Reading both and cross-checking them gives a fuller, more consistent picture than relying on one system.

Is this predictive?

No. Numerology and astrology are traditional, interpretive systems. Destiny OS uses documented methods and consistent rules, and offers guidance for reflection. It does not predict the future and is not a substitute for medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice.

What does "unlimited questions" mean?

Ask Destiny anything about your timing, decisions, colours or day, as often as you like, up to a fair-use limit of 30 questions a day.

What happens to my birth details?

They are used only to generate your guidance, never sold or shared, and you can delete your account and data at any time, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Can I cancel Destiny Plus?
