Timelines
Delivery clocks start once both the advance and a completed intake form are received. Delays in client feedback pause the clock.
Revisions
As listed per tier. Extra revision rounds cost ₹5,000 each. A change of brief after work starts is quoted as new work.
Cancellation and refunds
  • Before intake work begins: full refund of the advance.
  • After work begins: 50% of the advance is retained.

Rescheduling
Calls can be moved with 24 hours' notice. Missed calls without notice are counted as used.
Trademarks and domains
Checks are preliminary availability screens, not legal clearance. Please have a trademark attorney confirm the final name before adopting it. Domain, handle and registration costs are paid by the client.
Ownership
Once paid in full, the client owns the final selected name and taglines. Candidate names not selected remain with Destiny OS.
Confidentiality and data
Names, dates of birth and business details are used only for the engagement, never shared, and deleted on request, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
Nature of the service
Numerology is an interpretive system. Reports apply the documented Destiny OS method and are guidance, not a guarantee of business results, and are not a substitute for legal, financial or professional advice. All decisions rest with the client.
